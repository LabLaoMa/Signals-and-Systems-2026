## §6.2.1 Linear and Nonlinear Phase

1. Linear Phase

$$
\begin{aligned}
& H(j \omega)=e^{-j \omega t_{0}} \quad|H(j \omega)|=1 \quad \angle H(j \omega)=-\omega t_{0} \\
& \angle Y(j \omega)=\angle X(j \omega)-\omega t_{0}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
& x(t)=\cos (t / \sqrt{3})+\cos t+\cos \sqrt{3} t \\
& H(j \omega)=e^{-j \omega} \quad|H(j \omega)|=1 \quad \angle H(j \omega)=-\omega \\
& \left.\begin{array}{l}
\cos (t / \sqrt{3}) \rightarrow \frac{1}{2} e^{j t / \sqrt{3}} e^{-j / \sqrt{3}}+\frac{1}{2} e^{-j t / \sqrt{3}} e^{j / \sqrt{3}}=\cos ((t-1) / \sqrt{3}) \\
\cos (t) \rightarrow \frac{1}{2} e^{j t} e^{-j}+\frac{1}{2} e^{-j t} e^{j}=\cos (t-1) \\
\cos (\sqrt{3} t) \rightarrow \frac{1}{2} e^{j \sqrt{3} t} e^{-j \sqrt{3}}+\frac{1}{2} e^{-j \sqrt{3} t} e^{j \sqrt{3}}=\cos (\sqrt{3}(t-1)) \\
y(t)=\cos ((t-1) / \sqrt{3})+\cos (t-1)+\cos \sqrt{3}(t-1)
\end{array}\right\}
\end{aligned}
$$

2. Nonlinear Phase

$$
H(j \omega)=\frac{1-j \omega}{1+j \omega} \quad|H(j \omega)|=1 \quad \angle H(j \omega)=-2 \operatorname{tg}^{-1} \omega_{11}^{\omega}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
& \cos (t / \sqrt{3}) \rightarrow \frac{1}{2} e^{j t / \sqrt{3}} e^{-j 2 \operatorname{arctg}(\sqrt{3} / 3)}+\frac{1}{2} e^{-j t / \sqrt{3}} e^{j 2 \operatorname{arctg}(\sqrt{3} / 3) \pi / 3} \\
& \quad=\cos (t / \sqrt{3}-\pi / 3)
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
y(t)=\cos (t / \sqrt{3}-\pi / 3)+\cos (t-\pi / 2)+\cos (\sqrt{3} t-2 \pi / 3)
$$

$$

## §6．2．2 Group delay（群延时）

Consider a narrowband signal $\quad \Delta \omega \ll \omega_{0}$窄带信号
![](https://cdn.mathpix.com/cropped/1d9fb67a-1108-4630-8ab5-9b5f4fa7e767-13.jpg?height=424&width=591&top_left_y=202&top_left_x=1844)

$$
\begin{aligned}
& X(j \omega)=\left\{\begin{array}{ll}
\neq 0 & \left|\omega-\omega_{0}\right|<\frac{\Delta \omega}{2} \\
0 & \left|\omega-\omega_{0}\right|>\frac{\Delta \omega}{2}
\end{array} \quad \angle H(j \omega)=-\phi-\omega \alpha\right. \\
& Y(j \omega)=X(j \omega)|H(j \omega)| e^{-j \phi} e^{-j \omega \alpha} \quad \boldsymbol{\alpha}-\text { Group delay } \\
& \text { at } \boldsymbol{\omega}=\boldsymbol{\omega}_{0}
\end{aligned}
$$

The Group Delay at each frequency

$$
\tau(\omega)=-\frac{d}{d \omega}\{\angle H(j \omega)\}
$$

Example: $y(t)=x\left(t-t_{0}\right)$

$$
\begin{aligned}
& H(j \omega)=e^{-j \omega t_{0}} \\
& \angle H(j \omega)=-\omega t_{0} \\
& \tau(\omega)=t_{0} \quad(\text { signal delay })
\end{aligned}
$$

Example 6.1 Consider an all-pass system.

$$
\begin{aligned}
& H_{\mathrm{i}}(j \omega)=\frac{1+\left(j \omega / \omega_{i}\right)^{2}-2 j \zeta_{i}\left(\omega / \omega_{i}\right)}{1+\left(j \omega / \omega_{i}\right)^{2}+2 j \zeta_{i}\left(\omega / \omega_{i}\right)} \\
& \left|H_{\mathrm{i}}(j \omega)\right|=1 \Rightarrow|H(j \omega)|=1 \\
& \angle H_{\mathrm{i}}(j \omega)=-2 \operatorname{tg}^{-1}\left\{\frac{2 \zeta_{i}\left(\omega / \omega_{i}\right)}{1-\left(\omega / \omega_{i}\right)^{2}}\right\} \\
& \angle H(j \omega)=\sum_{\mathrm{i}=1}^{3} \angle H_{\mathrm{i}}(j \omega) \\
& \omega / \omega_{i}=f / f_{i} \quad \text { Let }\left\{\begin{array} { l } 
{ f _ { 1 } = 5 0 \mathrm { Hz } } \\
{ f _ { 2 } = 1 5 0 \mathrm { Hz } } \\
{ f _ { 3 } = 3 0 0 \mathrm { Hz } }
\end{array} \left\{\begin{array}{l}
\zeta_{1}=0.066 \\
\zeta_{2}=0.033 \\
\zeta_{3}=0.058
\end{array}\right.\right.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
H(j \omega)=\prod_{i=1}^{3} H_{\mathrm{i}}(j \omega)
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
& \tau \frac{d y(t)}{d t}+y(t)=x(t) \\
& H(j \omega)=\frac{1 / \tau}{j \omega+1 / \tau} \\
& |H(j \omega)|=\frac{1 / \tau}{\sqrt{\omega^{2}+(1 / \tau)^{2}}} \\
& 20 \lg |H(j \omega)|=20 \lg \left[1 /\left(\tau \sqrt{\omega^{2}+(1 / \tau)^{2}}\right)\right]
\end{aligned}
$$

(a) $\omega \ll 1 / \tau \quad 20 \lg |H(j \omega)| \approx 0$
(b) $\omega \gg 1 / \tau \quad 20 \lg |H(j \omega)| \approx-20 \lg \omega-20 \lg \tau$

It is approximately a linear function of $\lg (\boldsymbol{\omega})$

$-20 \lg \omega-20 \lg \tau=0 \quad \Rightarrow \omega=\frac{1}{\tau}$

$$

$$
